Frequently asked questions

Where does the account data come from?

Public and licensed sources: SEC EDGAR filings, GLEIF ownership records, Crunchbase, Diffbot, USPTO patents, Google News and enrichment providers. Each research run records which sources it used.

Will I see personal phone numbers or emails?

No. Only work email and phone details are shown. Personal emails and direct mobile numbers are removed on the server before any page receives them.

Can the copilot make things up?

It answers from your team's indexed research and cites the records it used. When the evidence isn't there, it says so instead of guessing.

Who can see which accounts?

Access is granted per account. Reps see the accounts they are assigned; administrators manage access and roles from the admin console.

How fresh is the information?

News, posts and job listings are refreshed by scheduled scrapes, and the Command Center flags when a feed hasn't been updated recently.
