Introduction Workflow

How an introduction progresses from idea to a live deal

Main Pipeline
1
Proposed
You or a connector suggests someone worth introducing to an account.
2
Requested
Formal ask is made — waiting for the connector to agree and make the introduction.
3
Accepted
Connector has agreed to make the intro. Waiting for them to connect both parties.
4
Intro Made
The connector has reached out and introduced both parties. Email thread started.
5
Meeting Held
First meeting happened. There is genuine interest — time to move to the deals pipeline.
Convert to Deal
Introduction graduates into an active deal in the pipeline.
Exit paths — can happen at any stage
Declined
Connector or contact explicitly said no. Introduction is closed.
Stale
No activity or response for an extended period. Follow up or close it.

Spawns a Deal
