Deals Pipeline Workflow

How a deal progresses from first contact to a closed win

Pipeline Stages
1
Intro
Deal created from a converted introduction. First conversations are happening.
2
Discovery
Understanding the prospect's needs, pain points and decision process.
3
Proposal
Formal proposal or SOW presented to the prospect. Negotiation begins.
Can win directly from here
4
Pilot
Running a limited proof-of-concept or trial to validate the solution.
5
Contract
Legal and procurement review. Signatures pending. Nearly at the finish line.
WON
Deal closed successfully. Relationship moves to delivery.
How deals close — win or loss
WON
Contract signed and deal is live. Can happen from Proposal, Pilot or Contract stage.
LOST
Prospect went with a competitor, budget cut, or deal fell through. Log the reason to improve future deals.
Key things to track on every deal
Next step & due date
Buying committee contacts
Deal value & expected close
Health score & attention flags
